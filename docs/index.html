Why you'd use it

Real ways to use WireGuard VPN

These examples show what the app can do and where it fits into an existing setup.

I need to reach a camera from another network

Join the camera to your WireGuard network, then use a configured proxy or port forward to reach its services.

I want to expose only a few services

Forward only the camera ports you need, or use the built-in HTTP CONNECT and SOCKS5 proxies.

I don't want to install a VPN driver

The app runs without root access or a kernel TUN device, using userspace networking.

How it works

A camera joins your VPN; you choose what can pass through

The camera connects to a WireGuard peer, while the app exposes selected camera services through proxies and port forwards.

Questions

Good to know

Does this route every camera app through the VPN?

No. The userspace build provides proxies and selected port forwards; it does not transparently route all firmware traffic.

Who can use the inbound SOCKS5 proxy?

Peers that can reach the camera over the VPN. Restrict VPN membership and keep camera services authenticated.

What if an old unsigned build is installed?

The README notes that upgrading to a signed build may require uninstalling the old package first.
